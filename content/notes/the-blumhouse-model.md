Keeping budgets reasonable and giving artists equity in their film has been shown to be a winning formula. That is exactly what we set out to do. Before Obsession came on the scene, we brought our most talented friends together to film Consumers. When Obsession became a massive hit, we knew that we had our fingers on the pulse and we were moving in the right direction. And now, it looks like Jason Blum is doubling down on his approach as the Hollywood Reporter released [this article](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/jason-blum-movies-million-1236517674/) recently.

This feels like wind in the sails. Onward to the next phase. We submitted Consumers to the Sundance Film Festival’s shorts program, and we are already looking forward to the feature. The indie spirit is alive and well!
